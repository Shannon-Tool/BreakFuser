# Code of conduct

## Our standards

We welcome constructive participation from people of every background and identity. Be respectful, assume good faith, give actionable feedback, and focus disagreements on technical merit. Credit others' work, respect privacy and confidentiality, and make space for differing experience levels.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and bad-faith accusations are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private information or use project spaces for spam or unlawful activity.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, releases, community discussions, and other project spaces, and when someone officially represents the project in public. It applies to maintainers and contributors alike.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **team@shannon-ai.com**, including relevant links and a concise description. Do not post sensitive reports publicly. The maintainers will respect reporters' privacy as far as practical and will not tolerate retaliation against good-faith reporters or witnesses.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and consistently, consider context and impact, and give affected parties an opportunity to respond when appropriate. Responses may include clarification, mediation, a warning, content removal, temporary restrictions, or loss of participation privileges. Enforcement will be proportionate and documented internally, with conflicts of interest recused where practical.

Maintainers may remove contributions or limit participation that violates this code. Project decisions remain focused on sustaining a safe, professional, and technically productive community.
